I am an operator-researcher building embodied intelligence for real-world
robots. My work spans tactile perception, dexterous manipulation, multimodal
learning, real-robot reinforcement learning, research infrastructure, and
recoverable robot-agent runtimes.

I received an M.Sc. in Sensor and Cognitive Psychology from Chemnitz University
of Technology and a B.Sc. in Photoelectronic Science and Technology from
Southwest University of Science and Technology. That path—from physics to
cognitive science to robotics—continues to shape how I think about intelligent
systems.

My central research question is:

> How can robots turn incomplete perception into grounded action—and keep
> correcting themselves as the world changes?
